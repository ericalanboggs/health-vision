## What to actually take from this

Two of these matter most for Summit:

**Mike Caulo** is the closest comparable that exists: same board certification,
coaching supported by his own app between sessions, mostly remote. That is
Summit's exact shape, already working at scale for someone else. Worth a real
teardown rather than a glance.

**Marisa Moon** is the argument for the SEO phase in
[`positioning-lead-with-eric.md`](./positioning-lead-with-eric.md). She won a
category on search by naming the niche plainly and repeating it everywhere, which
is close to free and does not depend on posting cadence. Summit has done none of
this yet.

**Fit Father Project** is the structural model for a men-40+ page specifically,
and is worth studying for *structure* while ignoring its claims: it leans hard on
body-composition outcome promises that `STRATEGIC_DIRECTION.md` §7 does not allow
in Summit's voice.

**Do not copy the funnel economics.** Several of these are funded or have a decade
of audience compounding behind them. The pattern worth copying is the page
structure: photo, credential, named audience, specific promise, proof, program,
one CTA repeated.
