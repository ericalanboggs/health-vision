# Coaching Client Quotes (1:1)

> Source of truth for testimonials from people who worked with Eric **directly, 1:1**.
> Collected 2026-10-27 from Eric. Verbatim as given.

**Keep this separate from `market-research-and-strategy/pilot-feedback.md`.** That
file holds *app pilot* users (Julie, Karin, Tim, AJ, Jesse), which is what
`summit-web/src/data/site.ts` → `TESTIMONIALS` draws from. The people below bought
Eric's time, not the app. Quoting an app user on `/coaching` would imply they bought
the Signature Plan; quoting a coaching client as an app testimonial implies the
reverse. One list each, no crossover.

## Rules for using these

- **Verbatim or clean excerpt only.** Trim from the ends or lift a whole sentence.
  Never splice two sentences into one, never reword, never fix their grammar.
  No ellipses mid-sentence in published copy — pick a sentence that stands alone.
- **The no-em-dash rule does not apply.** It governs *our* copy. A quote is theirs.
- **Specific results are safe in a client's own words** in a way they are never safe
  in ours (`summit-web/src/data/coaching.ts`, the note above `QUOTES`). "Increased my
  muscle mass" is Suze's claim about Suze. Do not restate it as a Summit outcome
  anywhere in our own voice — `STRATEGIC_DIRECTION.md` §7 still holds for us.
- **Roles are unconfirmed.** Nobody below supplied a job title. Do not invent one.
  Either get the real descriptor from Eric or render the attribution as the name
  and date alone.
- **Permission to publish is unconfirmed** for all five. Confirm before anything
  goes live with a name attached.
